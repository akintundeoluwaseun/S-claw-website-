Questions people ask.

Is S-CLAW really free?

Yes. No subscription, no account and no API key to buy. Download it and use it.

Which download do I pick?

Just pick your computer: Windows, Mac or Linux. The Download page picks it for you. S-CLAW then chooses the right brain for your computer's memory the first time it opens.

Do I need internet?

Only to download the app and its brain once. After that S-CLAW works on your computer without internet. No internet at all on that computer? Use “Installing on a computer with no internet?” on the Download page.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that safe?

That message appears for new apps that Windows doesn't know yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Can it do something without asking me?

Simple steps like opening an app, yes. Anything that can't be undone, like sending a message, deleting a file or submitting a form, waits for your approval, and the approval says exactly what will happen.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Desktop app on your computer, the same way you use it. No unofficial add-ons.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple M-series chips (M1 or newer). The first time, right-click S-CLAW in Applications and choose Open, because it isn't signed by Apple yet.

Is there a phone app?

An Android app is coming soon. For now S-CLAW runs on Windows, Mac and Linux computers.

Does it keep my passwords?

No. It refuses to save passwords, codes, keys or card numbers, and everything else stays on your own computer.
