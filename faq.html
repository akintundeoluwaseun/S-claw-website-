FAQ

Questions people ask

Is S-CLAW really free?

Yes. There's no subscription, no account and no API key to buy. Download it and use it.

Do I need internet?

Only to download the app and its brain once. After that S-CLAW thinks on your own computer and keeps working offline. No internet at all? Download a brain on another computer from the Download section and copy it over.

Can it do something without asking me?

Simple steps like opening an app, yes. Anything with consequences — sending a message, deleting a file, submitting a form — waits for your approval, and the approval quotes exactly what it will do.

Which download should I pick?

Just pick your computer: Windows, Mac or Linux. The app then offers the brain that suits your memory — Light for older laptops, Standard for most (8 GB), Pro for 16 GB or more.

Will it work with WhatsApp?

Yes — through the official WhatsApp Desktop app on your computer, the same way you use it. No unofficial add-ons.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, on Apple M-series Macs (M1 and newer). The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, right-click S-CLAW and choose Open.

Is there a phone app?

An Android app is coming soon.
