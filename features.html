Everything you can hand to S-CLAW.

If you can do it with your mouse and keyboard, you can ask S-CLAW to do it. Here are the kinds of things people say.

Messages and apps

Opens your apps, finds the right window, reads the screen, clicks and types.

  • Reply to Mum on WhatsApp: I'll call at 7

    You approve before it sends

  • Log this HP laptop repair in TechDesk

    Saved in TechDesk

The web

Searches, reads pages, fills forms and downloads files. It tells you plainly when it hits a login or a CAPTCHA.

  • Compare prices for an i5 laptop online

    Six options, side by side

  • Download the form from the school website

    Saved to your Downloads

Documents

PDFs (even scanned ones), Word, Excel and text. It tells you which words came from a scan and how sure it is.

  • Summarise this scanned PDF in five points

    Read from the scan, with a confidence note

  • Total this month's sales in Excel

    Added up and checked

Calendar and reminders

Creates events, finds free time and gives real reminders. Works with Outlook, Google Calendar and other calendar apps.

  • Book 30 minutes with Ada on Friday afternoon

    Friday, 2 PM, added

  • Remind me to call the supplier at 4

    Reminder set

  • What's on today?

    A morning briefing, once a day if you like

Voice and memory

Hold to talk and see what it heard before it starts. It remembers your preferences, projects and contacts, never passwords, codes or card numbers.

  • Remember that Tunde is my accountant

    Saved for next time

Scheduled jobs

Runs tasks later or every day, while S-CLAW is open in your taskbar.

  • Every evening, back up my Documents folder

    Runs daily at the time you choose

It thinks on your own computer.

S-CLAW's brain runs on your machine, so it's free, private and works offline after setup. When you first open it, it picks the brain that suits your computer's memory. You can change it later in Settings.
