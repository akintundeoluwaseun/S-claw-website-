Features

Everything S-CLAW can do for you

It operates the apps already on your computer, so it works with what you use every day.

Operates your apps

Opens apps, switches windows, reads the screen, clicks and types — WhatsApp, Word, your shop software, anything.

Works the web

Searches, reads pages, fills forms and downloads files — and tells you honestly when it hits a login or CAPTCHA.

Reads documents

PDFs (even scanned ones), Word, Excel and text. It shows which text came from a scan and how sure it is.

Changes documents, not just reads them

Join a month of invoices into one PDF, pull pages out, turn a sideways scan the right way up, stamp PAID, or shrink a file so it fits in an e-mail. The original is never touched.

The boring jobs, in bulk

Rename three hundred photos into the order they were taken. Shrink a folder of pictures for WhatsApp. Change a phone number across forty quotations. One letter per customer from a spreadsheet.

Installs what it needs

Missing a program for the job? S-CLAW can fetch it from the official Windows catalogue — after asking you, every time, and showing you exactly what it is about to install.

Calendar & reminders

Create events, find free time and get real reminders. Works with Outlook, Google and other calendar apps.

Morning briefing

Today's agenda and anything left unfinished, once a day — always saying what it can't see.

Voice

Push to talk. You see the transcript first, so nothing happens until it heard you right.

Remembers you

Your preferences, projects and contacts. Never passwords, codes or card numbers — it refuses those.

Scheduled jobs

Set tasks to run later or every day while S-CLAW is open, right from the tray.

Just ask

One sentence in. Real work out.

Private by design

Your computer. Your data. No bills.

S-CLAW Local thinks on your own machine. After setup it works without internet, and nothing you do leaves your computer.

  • No account, no sign-up, no API key
  • Free to download and use
  • Works offline after the first setup
  • Runs quietly in your taskbar with notifications
  • Light and dark mode
