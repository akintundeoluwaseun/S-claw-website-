How it works

You ask. It plans. You stay in charge.

S-CLAW turns one sentence into clear steps, does them on your computer, and checks each one.

  1. 1

    Type or say it

    Use the quick bar at the top of your screen: “Find last month's invoice PDF and email it to Tunde.”

  2. 2

    S-CLAW plans it

    It breaks the job into clear steps and shows you the plan before it starts.

  3. 3

    You approve what matters

    Sending, deleting or paying waits for you — and the approval says exactly what will happen, then every step is checked.

Built to be trusted

It works for you, never around you

Asks before it acts

Sending, deleting, paying or submitting always waits for you. The approval names the chat or file and quotes exactly what will happen.

Checks every step

After each action S-CLAW looks again to confirm it really worked — and tells you plainly when something didn't.

Keeps secrets out

It never saves passwords, codes, keys or card numbers. Everything else stays on your own computer.
