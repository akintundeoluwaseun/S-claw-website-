You ask. It works. You stay in charge.

Here's what happens from the moment you type a task to the moment it's done.

  1. You ask

    Press the quick bar at the top of your screen and type, or hold to talk. Plain English is fine.

    Email last month's invoice to Tunde

    The quick bar is always there, even with other apps open.

  2. It plans

    S-CLAW breaks the task into small, clear steps and shows them to you before it starts.

    1. 1Find the October invoice
    2. 2Attach the PDF to a new email
    3. 3Write a short message to Tunde

  3. It works and checks

    It uses your apps the way you would, then looks again after every step to make sure it really worked.

    1. ✓Found Invoice-October.pdfChecked
    2. ✓Attached to a new emailChecked
    3. Writing the messageWorking

  4. You approve

    Anything that can't be undone, like sending, deleting, paying or submitting, waits for you. The approval says exactly what will happen.
