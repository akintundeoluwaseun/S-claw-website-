What it can do

One assistant for the work you do on your computer

S-CLAW uses your apps the way you would — so it already works with what you have installed.

Operates your apps

Opens apps, switches windows, reads the screen, clicks and types — WhatsApp, Word, your shop software, anything.

Works the web

Searches, reads pages, fills forms and downloads files — and tells you honestly when it hits a login or CAPTCHA.

Reads documents

PDFs (even scanned ones), Word, Excel and text. It shows which text came from a scan and how sure it is.

Calendar & reminders

Create events, find free time and get real reminders. Works with Outlook, Google and other calendar apps.
