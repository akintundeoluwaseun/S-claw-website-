What it can do

One assistant for the work you do on your computer

S-CLAW uses your apps the way you would — so it already works with what you have installed.

Operates your apps

Opens apps, switches windows, reads the screen, clicks and types — WhatsApp, Word, your shop software, anything.

Works the web

Searches, reads pages, fills forms and downloads files — and tells you honestly when it hits a login or CAPTCHA.

Reads documents

PDFs (even scanned ones), Word, Excel and text. It shows which text came from a scan and how sure it is.

Calendar & reminders

Create events, find free time and get real reminders. Works with Outlook, Google and other calendar apps.

Morning briefing

Today's agenda and anything left unfinished, once a day — always saying what it can't see.

Voice

Push to talk. You see the transcript first, so nothing happens until it heard you right.

Remembers you

Your preferences, projects and contacts. Never passwords, codes or card numbers — it refuses those.

Scheduled jobs

Set tasks to run later or every day while S-CLAW is open, right from the tray.

How it works

Three steps. You stay in charge.

  1. 1

    Type or say it

    Use the quick bar at the top of your screen: “Find last month's invoice PDF and email it to Tunde.”

  2. 2

    S-CLAW plans it

    It breaks the job into clear steps and shows you the plan before it starts.

  3. 3

    You approve what matters

    Sending, deleting or paying waits for you — and the approval says exactly what will happen, then every step is checked.

  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. S-CLAW opens and sits in your taskbar. Press the quick bar and type your first task.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is S-CLAW really free?

Yes. There's no subscription, no account and no API key to buy. Download it and use it.

Do I need internet?

Only to download it. S-CLAW Local runs its brain on your computer, so it keeps working offline.

Can it do something without asking me?

Simple steps like opening an app, yes. Anything with consequences — sending a message, deleting a file, submitting a form — waits for your approval, and the approval quotes exactly what it will do.

Which download should I pick?

Standard for most laptops (8 GB of memory or more). Pro if you have 16 GB or more and want smarter, quicker answers.

Will it work with WhatsApp?

Yes — through the official WhatsApp Desktop app on your computer, the same way you use it. No unofficial add-ons.

Why is the Windows download so big?

The AI brain comes inside the installer, so you never need to download or set up anything else.
