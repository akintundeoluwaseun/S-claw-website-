Installing offline or on several PCs?

Most people can skip this — S-CLAW downloads its brain by itself on first start. Get a brain here only if the computer has no internet, or to set up many PCs without downloading again.

  1. Download every file for the brain you pick.
  2. Put them all in one folder.
  3. In S-CLAW open Settings → Claw's brain and choose the first file. S-CLAW joins the pieces itself.
